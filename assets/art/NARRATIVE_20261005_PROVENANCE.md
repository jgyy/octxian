# Bitter Wells native originals

Three independent images were generated on 2026-10-05 with ChatGPT image generation. Every call requested the highest supported native landscape resolution, with 1536×1024 or greater requested and no enlargement. The tool returned a separate **1536×1024 PNG** for each painting. The returned PNG bytes are committed directly, without cropping, resampling or recompression. A greater native size cannot be selected through this tool's available parameters.

All three use ordinary desert-town architecture and Chinese mineral-pigment/ink visual-novel styling, with no people or readable lettering. They were generated from distinct scene descriptions and were not edits of one another.

- **bitter_wells_caravan_workroom:** adobe workroom, two different weighted survey maps, bronze calibration tool, isolated lead, separate fresh/spent reference stones, cloth screen, kettle and tamarisk-lit window looking across the White Basin.
- **bitter_wells_workers_lodging:** shared meal and sleeping hall, beans and bread, clay bowls, bedding behind a woven divider, repaired sandals, travel packs and an evening cistern view.
- **bitter_wells_caravan_yard:** adobe cooperative yard, three covered carts blocked on level ground, checked harnesses, shaded stable rail, one calm mule, public cistern and distant salt ridges.

These are original environments. They add three to the original-background count and do not contribute to human, creature or outfit quotas. The existing expedition camp remains the setting for the caravan's road scenes.

The delivered native canvases are 1024×1536; those PNG bytes are retained without enlargement, cropping, mirroring or recolouring. The tool exposes no numeric resolution selector, so this record does not claim an 8K output.

Xu Lin's brief specified a deaf river mechanic with a gauge and slate; Chen Rui a fallible young advocate; Song Mei an older paid performer with a hand drum; Wu Zheng a retired weaver with a walking disability and his own tools. Wu's first draft received an anatomy correction in the same tool to place the crutch under his left arm with a visible supporting grip. The final output is counted as one original character, not two variants. The Tidemirror Otter and Cinderback Pangolin have independent anatomy, material detail and habitat interests. Neither design establishes an omen or grants a player ability.

The source path and delivered path identify the same final native PNG. CI checks canvas dimensions, raw file identity and decoded painting fingerprints. Originality beyond duplicate detection remains subject to editorial review.

## Planned earned transition

All scenes remain `4: Second pair` until common node `kiln_stage5_earned`, planned late in part20. Every later scene, including all endings, is `5: Four pairs`.

The locally adopted Azure Cloud method is agreed before any new-route admission at `kiln_p06_015` through `kiln_p06_022`. It requires at least 36 usable breath-units after ordinary work, sleep and sampling cost; at least 90% compatible charge; independent direction, interruption and grounded release through four primary inward/outward pairs; three calibrated examinations on separate recovered days; and normal following-day ordinary touch, grip and leg movement. Method agreement is a story event before training, not a retrospective change to the tests.

The first two pairs are the existing arm routes. The third is a distinct anterior lower-limb paired route from foot through knee to hip junction and return; the fourth is a distinct posterior heel/calf route to the hip junction and return. Each brief task spends half a unit over four ordinary breaths to deliver a quarter-unit of useful support, with input at most an eighth-unit per ordinary breath and any lower recovered or post-injury limit taking priority. Tasks are separated by full grounded release and rest.

No middle dantian, extraordinary-vessel bridge, great circuit, continuous talisman, flight or higher realm is opened. Ordinary fuel, material faults and injury recovery remain real constraints. Exact earned-node evidence and final independent-trial checkpoints will be documented once authored, rather than claiming the plan is already completed.

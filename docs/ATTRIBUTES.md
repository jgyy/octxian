# Cultivation attributes

Lin Yue develops **Qi Control, Physique, Comprehension and Dao Heart**. These are trained capacities, separate from her measured reserve, six paired meridian routes and earned realm. A high rank cannot unlock a missing anatomical route, heal an injury, supply fuel, purchase another person's agreement or establish a historical fact.

| Attribute | What practice earns it | What it cannot replace |
|---|---|---|
| Qi Control | Calibrated admission, bounded circulation and clean discharge | Reserve, purity, independent examinations |
| Physique | Recovered conditioning, ordinary footwork and safe carrying | Recovery, medicine, qi-powered legs |
| Comprehension | Technique study, source comparison and falsifiable observations | Untaken measurements, authorizations |
| Dao Heart | Resisting shortcuts, accepting limits and keeping freely made commitments | Consent, staff time, personal reputation |

Ranks describe experience at 0, 3, 6 and 10 points. Their final rank does not cap points. Choice previews show gains and requirements before selection. Every decision retains an accessible approach.

The version-1 save keys remain `qi`, `resolve`, `insight` and `trust`, respectively. Existing saves preserve exact values and scene IDs. Those historical field names are implementation details; the interface uses the cultivation meanings above. Historical scores are prior experience, not retroactive proof of new physical capacity.

Realm advancement still requires the authored recovered trials in [the cultivation canon](CULTIVATION_SYSTEM.md). The editorial audit records individual corrections to choices that previously rewarded administrative work with qi or conditioning, or treated another person's offered cooperation as a purchasable player stat.
